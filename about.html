About Holo Haus

Collecting meets infrastructure.

Holo Haus sits at the intersection of collecting, convenience, technology, and retail. We're built to run trading-card commerce quietly and reliably, inside the businesses people already visit.

Our mission

Make discovery easier. Make hosting effortless.

We exist to close the gap between people who want sealed trading-card product and the local businesses positioned to offer it. Collectors get a reliable, exciting place to find product. Retail partners get a new revenue stream without becoming a card shop.

What we run

Every operational layer, in-house

Sourcing and purchasing, product selection, inventory tracking, restocking logistics, machine maintenance, customer support, marketing, and sales monitoring, all handled by Holo Haus for every unit we place.

How we operate

Reliable, inventory-driven, professionally run.

Sourcing

Purchasing & selection

We buy and choose what goes into every machine based on real sell-through data, not guesswork.

Operations

Tracking & restocking

Every dispense is logged. Restocks are scheduled before a machine runs empty, not after.

Support

Maintenance & care

Machines are serviced on a routine schedule, with a support line for hosts and customers alike.

Built for the people on both sides of the machine.
